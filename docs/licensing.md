The app’s own code uses PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0. The app source repository is private. Public APK distribution does not grant public access to that source or rights to audiobook content, providers or trademarks. Third-party libraries, fonts, data and artwork retain their own terms.

A possible paid Play Store edition is exploratory. Commercial rights to contributions and third-party compliance must be reviewed before such a release. No contributor is assumed to have granted commercial rights by applying for access or accepting the app’s noncommercial license.

## This repository

The copied checker and Open Library manifest, and this repository’s original documentation, retain [PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0](../LICENSE.md) and the [required notice](../NOTICE). These terms are separate from the app binary and do not grant access to its source. This public toolkit is source available; do not describe it as permissively licensed or assume it permits commercial reuse. See [provenance](provenance.md) for the exact copied files.

The Open Library response fixture is third-party bibliographic data, not relicensed under PolyForm. [Open Library’s licensing statement](https://openlibrary.org/developers/licensing) says Internet Archive asserts no new proprietary rights over the database, while warning that existing rights issues may remain for some contributions or jurisdictions. Only two short records with IDs, titles and author names are retained; no covers or book text are bundled. This is not a blanket claim that all Open Library material is CC0.

You choose a license for an independently authored plugin in your own repository. Adapting copied material still requires compliance with its existing license. Submissions containing third-party code, data or assets must identify their applicable terms; maintainers cannot relicense other people’s work. A manifest never grants rights to the provider’s API, content, accounts or trademarks.
